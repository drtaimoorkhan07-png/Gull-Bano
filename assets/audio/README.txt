# Background Music Instructions

To play your own custom background song for Gull Bano:
1. Place your MP3 audio file in this directory (`assets/audio/`).
2. Name it `garden-ambient.mp3`.
3. When Gull Bano clicks the "Play Music" button on the site, it will play your special song!

NOTE: Even if you do not add an MP3 file, the site comes with a built-in soothing Web Audio harmonic harp synthesizer that automatically plays gentle romantic arpeggios when clicked!
